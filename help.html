Help & Contacts

In need of further assistance? Below are some useful contacts and resources.

EEMIS Listed Companies

EEMIS is an online platform by the Ministry of Gender, Labour and Social Develpment to monitor Ugandans working abroad. It provides a list of approved and vetted companies and approved jobs to Ugandans who wish to work abroad

Visit the EEMIS website ↗

MGLSD

Ministry of Gender Labour and Social Development (MGLSD) is a government department that focuses on improving working conditions, protecting vulnerable groups, and ensuring equal rights for men and women.

Visit the MGLSD website ↗
